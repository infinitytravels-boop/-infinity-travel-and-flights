BEFORE YOU PAY

Booking & payment information

Flight availability

Flight prices and seat availability can change until a booking is ticketed. A request is not a reservation.

Payment

Customers should pay only the final amount shown on the confirmed itinerary/payment request. Payment does not authorize us to change the agreed itinerary without confirmation.

Changes & refunds

Airline change, cancellation and refund rules depend on the fare purchased. Any service fees or applicable refund terms will be disclosed before payment.

Customer information

We use information submitted through this website to respond to travel requests and process bookings. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information through the request form.
